Professional Network

Kasbiy tarmoq

Connecting Uzbek-speaking healthcare professionals across the United States.

AQSh bo'ylab o'zbek tilida so'zlashuvchi sog'liqni saqlash mutaxassislarini bog'lash.

There are hundreds of Uzbek-speaking physicians, nurses, pharmacists, dentists, therapists, and allied health professionals practicing across America. Most don't know each other. There's no organized way to collaborate, mentor, refer patients, or build something together.

This network is being built to change that. The vision is simple: create a private professional community where Uzbek healthcare workers can connect across specialties, geographies, and career stages.

🤝

Mentorship

Senior physicians guiding residents and students from our community.

🔬

Collaboration

Research partnerships, case discussions, and shared clinical resources.

📋

Referrals

A trusted network for language-concordant patient referrals.

📢

Advocacy

A collective voice for immigrant health and diaspora medical needs.

What this will look like

Phase 1 (now): A private WhatsApp or Telegram group for verified healthcare professionals. Low friction, immediate value — ask questions, share opportunities, find collaborators.

Phase 2: A private online directory with profiles, specialties, and contact information — visible only to verified members.

Phase 3: Organized events — virtual case conferences, mentorship matching, annual meetup at a national medical conference.

We're starting with Phase 1. If you're an Uzbek-speaking healthcare professional practicing in the US, join now.

Bu qanday ko'rinadi

1-bosqich (hozir): Tasdiqlangan sog'liqni saqlash mutaxassislari uchun WhatsApp yoki Telegram guruhi. Savollar bering, imkoniyatlarni baham ko'ring, hamkorlarni toping.

2-bosqich: Profillar, mutaxassisliklar va kontakt ma'lumotlari bilan xususiy onlayn katalog.

3-bosqich: Uyushtirilgan tadbirlar — virtual holat konferensiyalari, murabbiylik juftlashuvi, yillik milliy tibbiy konferensiyada uchrashuv.

Biz 1-bosqichdan boshlaymiz. AQShda ishlaydigan o'zbek tilida so'zlashuvchi sog'liqni saqlash mutaxassisi bo'lsangiz, hoziroq qo'shiling.
