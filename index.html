About

Meet Otabek Pulatov

Otabek Pulatov bilan tanishing

I grew up in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, and moved to the United States alone at 19. Community college in Brooklyn, then PA school, then five years of clinical practice, then medical school at NYU. Patients trusted me before I had all the answers. Immigrant families reminded me of my own. Both changed how I practice.

I matched into Internal Medicine at UT Southwestern and am moving to Dallas. I'm leaning toward cardiology. Heart disease hits the communities I come from especially hard, and it's the work I'd like to spend my career doing.
