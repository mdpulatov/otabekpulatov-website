Why UzMedTalks exists

Nima uchun UzMedTalks yaratildi

There are over 500,000 Uzbek speakers in the United States. The overwhelming majority have limited access to health information in their own language — what little exists is either machine-translated or aimed at patients in Uzbekistan, where the healthcare system works completely differently.

AQShda 500,000 dan ortiq o‘zbek tilida so‘zlashuvchi bor. Ularning ko‘pchiligi o‘z tillarida sog‘liq ma‘lumotlariga cheklangan kirishga ega — mavjud bo‘lgan kam narsa mashinada tarjima qilingan yoki O‘zbekistondagi bemorlarga mo‘ljallangan.

UzMedTalks is my attempt to fill that gap — real medicine, real explanations, in the language our community actually speaks. No jargon. No rushing. No paywall.

UzMedTalks bu bo‘shliqni to‘ldirishga urinishim — haqiqiy tibbiyot, haqiqiy tushuntirishlar, jamoatimiz haqiqatan ham gaplashadigan tilda. Hech qanday atama. Hech qanday shoshilinch. Hech qanday to‘siq.

I’m Otabek Pulatov — I grew up in Tashkent, immigrated at 19, worked as a PA for five years across neurology and psychiatry, and now finish medical school at NYU and begin internal medicine residency in 2026. I built UzMedTalks because someone built it for me once, in a different form, and it changed my life.

Men Otabek Pulatov — Toshkentda o‘sdim, 19 yoshda muhojir bo‘ldim, nevrologiya va psixiatriyada besh yil PA sifatida ishladim va hozir NYUda tibbiyot maktabini tugatib, 2026-yilda ichki kasalliklar rezidenturasini boshlayman.
