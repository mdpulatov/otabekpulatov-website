How I Got Into Medical School

Tibbiyot maktabiga qanday kirdim

This is how I did it, and hopefully it helps if you're trying to do the same.

Men buni shunday qilganman, va shu yo'lni bosib o'tmoqchi bo'lganlarga foydali bo'lishiga umid qilaman.

Getting in was a dream come true, but the road there started years before any application went out.

Prerequisites, eight years later

Before I could even think about the MCAT, I had to go back and retake prerequisites I hadn't touched in almost a decade: physics, organic chemistry, biochemistry. Eight years out of undergrad, relearning material like that is its own kind of humbling, and so is getting used to being the oldest person in the room. If you're coming back to this after years away, budget real time for this step. It isn't a formality.

The MCAT

I took the MCAT and did well, above average, though a little below what I'd hoped for myself. It's worth being honest with yourself early about where your score realistically lands, because it shapes which programs are actually worth your time and money to apply to.

Where to apply

I moved to Texas hoping to get into medical school there. I liked the state, and the tuition was a lot cheaper. It didn't work out. Not a single Texas school called me for an interview, most likely because of the minimum residency requirement a lot of state schools have. If tuition is driving your state choice, check the residency timeline before you move, not after.

I applied to programs across Texas and New York and ended up interviewing at three schools in New York: Downstate, NYU Long Island, and NYMC.

The interviews

Don't assume every interview looks the same. The NYU Long Island interview had two parts. The one-on-one went well, their mission was close to something I actually believed, so my answers came out genuine. The second part was a group activity, and I completely blew it. Everyone else seemed to know the games we were supposed to play. I didn't grow up here, I'd never played charades in my life. I did what I could with it and hoped that would be enough. It was.

What I'd tell someone starting this now

Prerequisites take longer than you remember. If it's been years, treat physics, orgo, and biochem like a real semester, not a refresher.

Know your MCAT score honestly. Apply where it actually gives you a shot, not just where you want to go.

Check residency requirements before you relocate for cheaper tuition. A move doesn't guarantee in-state status.

Ask what the interview format actually is. Some are just conversations. Some include group activities you can't fully prepare for. Show up anyway.

A coherent story matters as much as your numbers. Faculty are reading for a consistent narrative before they ever meet you. Make sure yours holds together.

And a little luck helps too. That part never fully goes away.

Prerekvizitlar, sakkiz yildan keyin

MCAT haqida o'ylashdan oldin, deyarli o'n yil qo'l tekkizmagan fanlarni qayta olishim kerak edi: fizika, organik kimyo, biokimyo. Bakalavriatdan sakkiz yil o'tgach bunday materialni qayta o'rganish o'ziga xos kamtarlik saboqi, xuddi sinfdagi eng katta yoshli odam bo'lishga o'rganish kabi. Agar yillar davomida uzoqlashgan bo'lsangiz, bu qadam uchun haqiqiy vaqt ajrating. Bu shunchaki formallik emas.

MCAT

MCAT topshirdim va yaxshi natija oldim, o'rtachadan yuqori, lekin o'zim kutganimdan biroz past. O'z ballingiz haqiqatan qayerda turishini erta va halol tan olish muhim, chunki bu qaysi dasturlarga ariza berish arziydigan bo'lishini belgilaydi.

Qayerga ariza berish kerak

Texasga ko'chib o'tdim, u yerda tibbiyot maktabiga kirish umidida. Bu shtatni yoqtirardim, va o'qish puli ancha arzon edi. Bu ishlamadi. Texasdagi hech bir maktab meni intervyuga chaqirmadi, ehtimol ko'p shtat maktablarida bo'lgan minimal rezidentlik talabi tufayli. Agar o'qish puli sizni shtat tanlashga undayotgan bo'lsa, ko'chib o'tishdan oldin rezidentlik muddatini tekshiring, keyin emas.

Texas va Nyu-Yorkdagi dasturlarga hujjat topshirdim va oxir-oqibat Nyu-Yorkdagi uchta maktabda intervyu berdim: Downstate, NYU Long Island va NYMC.

Intervyular

Har bir intervyu bir xil ko'rinadi deb o'ylamang. NYU Long Island intervyusi ikki qismdan iborat edi. Yakka tartibdagi suhbat yaxshi o'tdi, ularning missiyasi menga yaqin edi, shuning uchun javoblarim samimiy chiqdi. Ikkinchi qism guruh faoliyati edi va men uni butunlay barbod qildim. Hamma boshqalar o'ynashimiz kerak bo'lgan o'yinlarni bilardek tuyulardi. Men bu yerda o'smaganman, umrimda charades o'ynamagandim. Qo'limdan kelganini qildim va shu yetarli bo'lishiga umid qildim. Va yetarli bo'ldi.

Hozir buni boshlayotganlarga aytadiganlarim

Prerekvizitlar esingizdagidan ko'proq vaqt oladi. Agar yillar o'tgan bo'lsa, fizika, organik kimyo va biokimyoni haqiqiy semestr sifatida qabul qiling, shunchaki takrorlash sifatida emas.

MCAT ballingizni halol biling. Sizga haqiqatan imkoniyat beradigan joylarga ariza bering, faqat borishni xohlagan joyingizga emas.

Arzonroq o'qish puli uchun ko'chishdan oldin rezidentlik talablarini tekshiring. Ko'chish shtat maqomini kafolatlamaydi.

Intervyu formati qanday ekanini so'rang. Ba'zilari shunchaki suhbat. Ba'zilarida to'liq tayyorlanib bo'lmaydigan guruh faoliyatlari bor. Baribir boring.

Bir butun hikoya raqamlaringiz kabi muhim. Fakultet sizni uchratishdan oldin izchil hikoya izlaydi. O'zingiznikini mustahkam qiling.

Va biroz omad ham yordam beradi. Bu qism hech qachon to'liq yo'qolmaydi.
